What this is

We joined Meet5 the way a new member does, then held your paywall up against the report.

Adapty runs subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and State of Paywalls 2026 is what we learned from 78,000+ of their paywalls. We installed Meet5, signed up, went through your onboarding to the Premium screen, opened Choose your plan from the profile, and read your App Store listing and your Premium page. Then we compared each part of your paywall with the report.

Benchmarks are for Lifestyle, your App Store category, and Europe, your home market. Each idea below is built into a clickable prototype, in your design.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: the gold arc, the meet5 premium mark, your gold icons and ticks, your Premium-M and Premium-L cards and your gold button. Only what the paywall says and which plan it leads with changed.

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes first, wording second: the report found that changes to plans and offers win A/B tests more often than visual or copy changes. Each idea links to its screen in the prototype.

01

Lead with 12 months, like your website does

Your first Premium screen has one button: 6 months for 98,99 €. On Choose your plan, 6 months is pre-selected too. Your Premium page opens on 12,49 € a month, billed yearly. Show both plans and start on 12 months.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · Changing plan duration raised LTV in 59% of A/B tests. Visual or copy changes did so in 35%. See the screen 59% of testsraised LTV
plan-duration tests
02

Offer 6 months when someone says no

Tap the X today and you are back in the app. Show one more screen first: the 6-month plan you already sell at 98,99 €, for people who are not ready to pay for a full year.

03

Put your 4.7 stars and your members’ words on the paywall

Two screens into sign-up you show 4.7/5 stars, 26,000+ five-star ratings and reviews from members like Tim and Angela. None of it is on the paywall, where people decide.

Greet them by name and city

By the time the paywall opens, Meet5 knows their first name, their city and how often they want to go out. The headline still says “Experience more as a Premium Member!” to everyone. Try “Lars, join Frankfurt activities first.”
